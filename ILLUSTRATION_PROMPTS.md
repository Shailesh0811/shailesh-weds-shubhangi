# AI Illustration Prompts — Shailesh & Shubhangi Wedding
## Style Reference: 2D Cartoon Big-Eye Indian Couple

The style you want is exactly like the reference image:
- **2D semi-realistic cartoon** (NOT 3D chibi)
- Very **large expressive eyes** with white shine dots
- **Man**: short black beard, warm brown skin, white/cream shirt, dark trousers, warm smile
- **Woman**: curly/wavy dark hair, warm brown skin, vibrant blue saree with gold border, bindi, nose ring, pearl necklace, expressive big eyes
- Proportions: slightly larger heads, realistic bodies
- Warm bokeh/floral background or transparent
- Style similar to: Pixar/Disney 2D concept art, Indian cartoon portrait style

---

## HOW TO GENERATE (Step by Step)

### Option 1 — Midjourney (Best Quality)
1. Go to https://midjourney.com
2. Upload your groom photo + bride photo to Discord
3. Copy their image URLs
4. Use: `/imagine [your prompt] --cref [groom_url] [bride_url] --cw 80 --ar 1:1 --v 6`

### Option 2 — DALL-E 3 via ChatGPT Plus
1. Open ChatGPT with GPT-4o
2. Upload both reference photos
3. Say: "Using these reference photos, generate a cartoon couple illustration in this exact style: [paste prompt]"

### Option 3 — Adobe Firefly (Transparent background support)
1. Go to firefly.adobe.com
2. Use Text to Image with the prompts below
3. Enable "Transparent background"

### Option 4 — Canva AI / Bing Image Creator (Free)
- Paste the prompt directly

## TIPS

### Make all illustrations consistent (Midjourney)
After generating illustration #1, use `--cref` to keep the same character design:
```
[next prompt] --cref https://cdn.midjourney.com/[your-image-url] --cw 80
```

### Remove background
- Use **remove.bg** (free) or **Adobe Firefly** (native transparency)
- Or in Photoshop: Select Subject → Delete Background
